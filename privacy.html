    Privacy policy.

    Last updated: 4 November 2026

    This policy explains what information Nexora Labs may receive when you visit this website or contact us, and how it is used. We keep this explanation practical and limited to the way this site currently works.

    Information you choose to share

    If you use the project inquiry form, the details you enter are used to prepare an email in your own email application. The form does not submit your message to a database on this website. Your email provider may process the message under its own privacy terms.

    If you contact us directly by email, phone, or WhatsApp, we receive the information you include in that conversation and use it to reply and discuss your inquiry.

    Website data and cookies

    This website does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, or a website contact-form database. We do not intentionally set tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as a browser request or IP address, to deliver and protect the website; please consult the provider's own privacy information for its practices.

    Third-party services and links

    The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from a third-party content delivery service. Opening WhatsApp, your email application, or any other external link takes you to a separate service with its own privacy policy and data practices. We do not control those services.

    How information is used and shared

    We use information you send to respond, understand a potential project, and communicate with you. We do not sell your contact details. We share information only when needed to provide a service you request, operate the site through its providers, or meet a legal obligation.

    Retention and your choices

    Messages may be retained in our communication accounts for as long as needed to respond, manage a business relationship, or meet legal requirements. You can ask us to correct or delete information you have sent, subject to any legal or operational reason we must retain it.

    Children and policy updates

    This website is intended for businesses and general audiences, not children. We may update this policy when the website or our practices change; the date above indicates the latest revision.
